Delete your Mulligan Hills account

Mulligan Hills uses a random anonymous account for cloud save and the daily challenge. It has no name or email address. Use this page to delete that account and its data.

What is deleted

  • Your cloud save files.
  • Your daily challenge scores.
  • Your anonymous account and any unused transfer code.
  • Optional usage statistics linked to your install ID, if you enter it below.

What is not deleted

  • Saves stored on your own phone (delete the app or its data to remove them).
  • Your purchase. It belongs to your Google Play or Apple account, and deleting the account does not refund or cancel it. You can restore it later.

Deleting cannot be undone.

How to prove the account is yours

Because accounts are anonymous, the only proof is a one-time code made inside the game: open the game, go to Settings > Delete account > Get a code [CONFIRM the final menu text], and enter the code here within 15 minutes.

If you cannot open the game any more, email SUPPORT_EMAIL and we will explain what we can do. [LAWYER: wording; we cannot identify an anonymous account without a code.]

# Parody pro and celebrity roster — 100-character concept draft

Date: 5 October 2026. Nathan requested 50 modern-era pro inspirations and 50 widely recognisable celebrity/music inspirations for an audience aged 25–50. These are fictional character concepts, not licensed likenesses, implementation data or cleared release names. Recognition is an editorial hypothesis, not a surveyed audience result. Inspirations remain internal; names/designs need review before public use. No celebrity endorsement is implied.

All outfits, equipment and carts must be generated procedurally under DEC-062. Use original shapes, palettes, patterns, dialogue and sound; no downloaded likenesses, copied logos, signature recordings, lyrics, franchise costumes or branded vehicle badges. Broad performance/fashion tropes provide the joke. These ideas describe fictional gameplay, not allegations about real people. Changing a name alone does not establish release clearance.

## Roster structure

35 male and 15 female modern-era golf inspirations; Tiger is explicitly a rare legend guest rather than an assertion that he is a current full-time tour player. Celebrity mix: 30 music acts and 20 screen/entertainment personalities, combining established stars with younger recognisable names. Celebrity ages are not restricted to 25–50: that is the intended player audience. Every proposed name is provisional; non-English names must be checked by native speakers for unintended meanings and awkward jokes.

## Integration and production rules

- Pros offer optional earned-cash private matches and skill challenges under DEC-072; no automatic stake commitment. Give each distinct attribute strengths and a meaningful route choice, not merely stronger numbers at every tier. Listed challenges are concepts, not implemented mechanics.
- Celebrities can visit, react to the course, become VIPs and purchase an available home under DEC-073. No visitor bypasses home capacity or becomes a mandatory building gate. They may offer friendly challenges; celebrity fame does not imply pro-level golf skill.
- Luck affects eligible encounter frequency, pro offers and positive breaks as described in `../golfer_controls.md`. Course/reputation/unlock conditions, independent outcome rules, cooldowns and outstanding-offer caps still apply. A famous visitor is a story opportunity, not a guaranteed reward.
- Distinct characters are content definitions over shared procedural rig/outfit/bag/cart systems. Do not build 100 independent animation rigs or run 100 visitors simultaneously. Start with a representative test batch; stage additional definitions without cutting required golfer/club systems.
- Cosmetic ownership and appearance selection remain separate. NPC equipment skins do not automatically become player rewards; reward eligibility needs explicit definitions. Cosmetic models do not change sim colliders, golf attributes, cash or rating. Original visual victory rewards must follow DEC-074.
- Gags are occasional and nonblocking; repeated every stop would become irritating. Lion Woods' suggested black luxury SUV cart is an original unbranded design. Nathan approved the visual gag: occasionally show the cart upside down near Lion Woods and his ball. No explosion or injury, and no real crash reconstruction. Use a scripted presentation pose without collision physics, progress loss or score changes. The next departure restores the upright pose. Frequency remains to be tuned; do not repeat at every stop.
- Keep real inspiration references out of player-facing UI and runtime identifiers. Stable fictional IDs, deterministic visitor/event seeds and saved settlement IDs are required before wiring the roster into gameplay. Content schema, stats, unlocks, encounter rates and name clearance remain open.
